AWR Browser

Privacy Policy

A free browser for Hong Kong and Macao, intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Effective: 10 November 2026

Local data

Bookmarks are encrypted on your device. Their key is stored in the local Keychain without Keychain cloud synchronization; the bookmark database is excluded from system cloud backups. The app has no bookmark upload server or account synchronization service. Preferences, including language, filtering options and website exceptions, are stored locally.

Passwords and sharing

The app access password and QR sharing passwords are separate. QR encryption and decryption happen locally. QR sharing passwords are not retained or uploaded by the app; if forgotten, export again. Ordinary bookmark file export is not encrypted.

Website connections

Browsing, searching and playing online videos send ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and requests, to the selected websites. Websites may use cookies and website storage under their own policies. Google provides the default search. Filtering does not guarantee removal of all ads or tracking.

Advertising filtering

Bundled EasyList, EasyList China and host rules are processed using WebKit rules and local page-element handling. Filtering takes place on the device; visited URLs are not uploaded to analyze filtering results. Some advanced filtering syntax is unsupported.

Permissions

Camera access is used only when you choose to scan. If declined, you can still choose QR images or files. System pickers read the photos and files you select. The app does not request location permission.

User-directed exports

Files or images you choose to share or save are managed by the selected destination and your system cloud-sync settings. The app cannot remove copies shared elsewhere. Send sharing passwords separately.

Managing and deleting data

Manage bookmarks inside the app. Confirming removal of the app password clears local bookmarks and open tabs and cannot be undone. Clearing cache preserves bookmarks, passwords and sign-in state; it does not delete all website data. The operating system manages Keychain items; uninstalling does not guarantee removal of every Keychain item.

Age and contact

This product is intended for adults aged 18 and over. A store age rating does not mean the app has verified your identity. The app is provided by the individual developer identified on its App Store listing. Contact the support email below for privacy questions.
