AWR Browser

Support

A free browser for Hong Kong and Macao, intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Effective: 10 November 2026

Add bookmarks

Open a webpage, choose Add bookmark, confirm its name and save. You can also add an address from Home.

Encrypted QR sharing

Select 1–5 bookmarks, set a sharing password of at least 8 characters and export. The recipient scans or selects the image, enters the same password and confirms import. Send the password separately.

App password

Optionally set an access password of at least 6 characters. It differs from sharing passwords. Removing it requires confirmation and clears bookmarks and open tabs.

Filtering and exceptions

Manage the global switch and website exceptions under My > Ad blocking. Add an exception if a website does not display correctly. Some advanced syntax is unsupported.

Video playback

Play local files, direct video URLs and extractable ordinary web videos. DRM, blob/MSE and some cross-origin videos must remain in the original webpage.

Report a problem

Email your app version, iOS version, steps and expected result. Do not send access passwords, sharing passwords, private bookmarks or your complete browsing history.
